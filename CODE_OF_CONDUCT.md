# Code of Conduct

AccessLab welcomes contributors of all backgrounds, abilities and experience levels. This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces, and to interactions while representing the project.

Be respectful and constructive. Critique work rather than people, explain disagreements, respect privacy and communication preferences, and provide accessible text descriptions for visual evidence. Do not demand that someone disclose a disability or personal experience to justify feedback.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable.

The maintainer, [@TemoSulava](https://github.com/TemoSulava), handles reports. Use a private channel you already share with the maintainer. If none is available, open an issue asking for a private contact without naming people or sharing incident details. Do not use vulnerability reporting for conduct complaints. Anyone affected may stop participating or block contact while a report is considered.

Maintainers will consider the context and impact, protect reporter privacy where possible, and may edit or remove content, warn a participant, restrict participation temporarily or permanently, or block an account. There is no promised response time. A report involving the sole maintainer currently has no independent project appeals body; GitHub's abuse reporting remains available for platform violations.
